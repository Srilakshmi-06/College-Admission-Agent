COURSE SELECTION GUIDANCE
=============================================================

For students interested in Artificial Intelligence:
- B.Tech CSE with AI/ML specialization is highly recommended
- B.Tech Artificial Intelligence and Data Science (AIDS) is the most focused option
- B.Sc Data Science is a good option for those preferring a 3-year programme
- M.Tech Artificial Intelligence is available for postgraduate study

For students interested in Software Development:
- B.Tech CSE is the most comprehensive programme
- BCA is a good entry point requiring less strict eligibility

For students with strong Mathematics background:
- All engineering programmes welcome strong mathematics students
- B.Sc Data Science and M.Sc Data Science are mathematics-intensive

For students interested in Electronics:
- B.Tech ECE covers embedded systems, IoT, and communication technologies
