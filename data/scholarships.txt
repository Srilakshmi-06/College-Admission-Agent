SCHOLARSHIPS AND FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE — SAMPLE GUIDE
Greenfield Institute of Technology and Management
Academic Year 2024-2025

NOTE: This is a SAMPLE/DEMO document. All scholarship details are illustrative only.

=============================================================
MERIT SCHOLARSHIPS
=============================================================

1. Director's Merit Scholarship
   Eligibility: Top 5 students in each B.Tech branch based on JEE Main / GITM-ET rank
   Benefit: 100% tuition fee waiver for the first year
   Renewal: Maintain CGPA of 8.5 or above each semester
   No. of Scholarships: 5 per branch (25 total for B.Tech)

2. Academic Excellence Scholarship
   Eligibility: Students scoring 90% or above in Class 12 board exams
   Benefit: 50% tuition fee waiver for the first year
   Renewal: Maintain CGPA of 8.0 or above each semester
   No. of Scholarships: 10 per batch

3. Gold Medallist Scholarship
   Eligibility: Students with Class 12 state board topper / Gold Medal
   Benefit: Full tuition waiver for all 4 years (subject to performance)
   Verification: State board certificate required

=============================================================
NEED-BASED FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE
=============================================================

4. Financial Assistance Programme (FAP)
   Eligibility:
   - Annual family income below INR 2,50,000 (as per income certificate)
   - Must submit income certificate from competent authority
   - Must maintain academic performance (CGPA 6.5 minimum)
   Benefit: Up to 75% tuition fee concession
   Documentation required: Income certificate, BPL card (if applicable)

5. Single Parent Support Scholarship
   Eligibility: Students from single-parent families with income below INR 3,50,000
   Benefit: 30% tuition fee reduction
   Documentation: Legal proof of single-parent status, income certificate

=============================================================
GOVERNMENT SCHOLARSHIPS (External — Facilitated by Institution)
=============================================================

6. Post Matric Scholarship (SC/ST/OBC Students)
   - Government of India scholarship for SC, ST, OBC students
   - Institution facilitates application through National Scholarship Portal
   - Amount varies as per government norms
   - Apply at: scholarships.gov.in

7. Central Sector Scholarship Scheme
   - For students from families with income below INR 8,00,000 per year
   - Administered by Ministry of Education
   - Students apply directly through National Scholarship Portal

8. State Government Scholarships
   - Various state government scholarships available
   - Institution assists with documentation
   - Refer to your respective state's scholarship portal

=============================================================
SPORTS AND CULTURAL SCHOLARSHIPS
=============================================================

9. Sports Excellence Scholarship
   Eligibility: National/State level sports achievement
   Benefit: 25% to 50% tuition fee reduction based on achievement level
   Required: Certificate from recognised sports federation

10. Cultural Achievement Scholarship
    Eligibility: National level achievement in arts, music, or cultural activities
    Benefit: 15% tuition fee reduction
    Required: Certificate of achievement

=============================================================
SCHOLARSHIP APPLICATION PROCESS
=============================================================

Step 1: Download the scholarship application form from the college website
Step 2: Fill in the form with accurate personal and academic details
Step 3: Attach all required supporting documents (see checklist below)
Step 4: Submit to the Scholarship Cell (Room 201, Admin Block)
Step 5: Scholarship Committee review (within 30 days)
Step 6: Award letter issued to selected candidates
Step 7: Scholarship amount adjusted against fee payment

Required Documents for Scholarship Application:
- Completed application form
- Class 10 and 12 mark sheets (certified copies)
- Income certificate (for need-based scholarships)
- Community certificate (for category-based scholarships)
- Bank account details (for direct scholarship credit)
- Passport-size photograph (2 copies)
