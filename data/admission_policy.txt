=============================================================
ADMISSION POLICY OVERVIEW
=============================================================

Greenfield Institute of Technology and Management (GITM) follows a transparent,
merit-based admission process. Admissions are governed by:
- The institution's own admission rules
- University Grants Commission (UGC) guidelines
- State Government reservation policies
- AICTE norms (for technical programmes)

=============================================================
RESERVATION POLICY
=============================================================

Horizontal Reservations (applicable across all categories):
  Physically Challenged (PwD)    : 3%
  Sports Quota                   : 2%

Vertical Reservations (state government norms — SAMPLE):
  Scheduled Caste (SC)           : 15%
  Scheduled Tribe (ST)           : 7.5%
  Other Backward Classes (OBC-NCL): 27%
  Economically Weaker Section (EWS): 10%
  General Category (Open Merit)  : 40.5%

Note: Reservations are subject to State Government norms. The actual
reservation percentages may vary based on state regulations. Refer to
the official notification for applicable reservation policy.

=============================================================
ADMISSION PROCESS
=============================================================

Step 1 — Online Registration
  - Visit the admissions portal: admissions.gitm.ac.in (SAMPLE URL)
  - Create an account with a valid email address and mobile number
  - Fill in the online application form

Step 2 — Application Fee Payment
  - Pay the application fee online (INR 500 for general, INR 250 for SC/ST)
  - Application fee is non-refundable

Step 3 — Entrance Test (if applicable)
  - Download the hall ticket from the admissions portal
  - Appear for GITM-ET on the scheduled date
  - JEE Main qualified students may be exempted

Step 4 — Merit List
  - Merit is calculated based on:
    - JEE Main score (if submitted): 60% weightage
    - Class 12 marks: 40% weightage
    - For students appearing GITM-ET: GITM-ET score used instead of JEE

Step 5 — Counselling
  - Shortlisted students are called for online/offline counselling
  - Students choose preferred courses and branches
  - Seats are allotted based on merit rank and availability

Step 6 — Document Verification
  - Original documents must be produced for verification
  - See documents_required.txt for the complete checklist

Step 7 — Fee Payment and Admission Confirmation
  - Pay the prescribed tuition and other fees within the deadline
  - Admission is confirmed only after fee payment
  - Collect the admission letter and ID card from the Admissions Office

=============================================================
CANCELLATION AND REFUND POLICY
=============================================================

Cancellation before the start of the academic session:
  - Caution deposit (INR 5,000) is refunded
  - Tuition fee refund as per UGC guidelines:
    - Cancellation 15+ days before classes: Full refund minus INR 1,000 processing fee
    - Cancellation 8–14 days before classes: 80% of tuition refunded
    - Cancellation less than 8 days before classes: 50% of tuition refunded
    - After classes begin: No refund of tuition fee

=============================================================
CODE OF CONDUCT
=============================================================

Students admitted to GITM must:
- Adhere to the institution's Code of Conduct
- Sign the Anti-Ragging Undertaking (mandatory under Supreme Court order)
- Maintain minimum attendance (75% per subject)
- Follow the academic calendar

Violation of the Code of Conduct may result in disciplinary action,
including suspension or expulsion.

=============================================================
LATERAL ENTRY ADMISSIONS
=============================================================

Lateral entry to the 2nd year (3rd semester) of B.Tech:
  Eligible candidates: Diploma holders in relevant engineering discipline
  Minimum marks: 60% in Diploma
  Seats available: Up to 10% of sanctioned intake (lateral entry quota)
  Process: Separate lateral entry merit list published
  Direct admission without entrance test for lateral entry
