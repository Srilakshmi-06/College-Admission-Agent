=============================================================
UNDERGRADUATE ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
=============================================================

B.Tech Programmes (All Branches including CSE, AIDS, ECE, ME, CE):
- Minimum Academic Qualification: Class 12 (10+2) or equivalent
- Required Subjects: Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry/Computer Science/Biology
  (Physics and Mathematics are mandatory for all B.Tech programmes)
- Minimum Marks: 60% aggregate in PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics)
  or PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology) or PCMC (Physics, Chemistry, Maths, Computer Science)
- For SC/ST/OBC categories: Minimum 55% aggregate (as per government norms)
- Age Limit: Minimum 17 years as of December 31 of the admission year

Entrance Examination:
- JEE Main score is accepted for merit-based admission
- The institution also conducts its own entrance test: GITM-ET (Greenfield Entrance Test)
- Students with JEE Main score above the cutoff may be directly admitted
- Students without JEE Main score must appear for GITM-ET

B.Sc Data Science and Analytics:
- Minimum: Class 12 with Mathematics as a subject
- Minimum 55% aggregate marks
- No separate entrance exam required

BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications):
- Minimum: Class 12 in any stream with Mathematics
- Minimum 50% aggregate marks
- No separate entrance exam required

=============================================================
POSTGRADUATE ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
=============================================================

M.Tech — Computer Science and Engineering:
- B.Tech/B.E. in Computer Science, IT, or related field
- Minimum 60% aggregate (or 6.5 CGPA)
- GATE score preferred but not mandatory
- Valid GATE score holders get scholarship preference

M.Tech — Artificial Intelligence:
- B.Tech/B.E. in CSE, IT, ECE, or B.Sc in Computer Science/Mathematics/Statistics
- Minimum 60% aggregate (or 6.5 CGPA)
- GATE score preferred

MBA — Master of Business Administration:
- Any undergraduate degree (minimum 3 years) from a recognised university
- Minimum 50% aggregate marks
- CAT/MAT/XAT score accepted for merit shortlisting
- Group Discussion (GD) and Personal Interview (PI) are part of the selection process

M.Sc — Data Science:
- B.Sc / B.Tech in Mathematics, Statistics, Computer Science, or related field
- Minimum 55% aggregate marks

=============================================================
SPECIAL CATEGORIES
=============================================================

NRI/Foreign Students:
- Equivalent qualifications from recognised foreign boards accepted
- Contact the International Admissions Office for specific requirements

Persons with Disabilities (PwD):
- 3% horizontal reservation as per Government of India norms
- Minimum eligibility criteria as per UGC guidelines

Sports Quota:
- Up to 2% seats reserved for outstanding sports persons
- Must provide valid sports achievement certificate from recognised federation
