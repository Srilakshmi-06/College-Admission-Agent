GENERAL ADMISSION QUESTIONS
=============================================================

Q: When does the admission process start?
A: The online application portal opens on 1st March 2025 for undergraduate programmes
   and 15th March 2025 for postgraduate programmes (sample dates).

Q: Is there an entrance exam?
A: Yes. The institution conducts its own entrance test called GITM-ET.
   Students with valid JEE Main scores may be eligible for direct admission
   based on JEE merit. Students without JEE scores must appear for GITM-ET.

Q: How do I apply?
A: Applications are accepted online through the admissions portal.
   Register on the portal, fill in the application form, upload documents,
   and pay the application fee. See the Admission Policy for the full process.

Q: Can I apply for multiple courses?
A: Yes, you can indicate your course preferences during the online application.
   You may list up to 3 course preferences in order of priority.

Q: What is the application fee?
A: The application fee is INR 500 for General category and INR 250 for SC/ST
   candidates (sample amounts). The application fee is non-refundable.

COURSES AND ELIGIBILITY QUESTIONS
=============================================================

Q: What is the minimum percentage required for B.Tech admission?
A: Minimum 60% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM)
   in Class 12 board examinations. SC/ST candidates: 55%.

Q: Is Mathematics mandatory for B.Tech?
A: Yes. Mathematics and Physics are mandatory subjects for all B.Tech programmes.

Q: I have Biology instead of Computer Science in Class 12. Can I still apply?
A: Yes, if you have Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics. Biology is not a
   required subject for B.Tech engineering programmes.

Q: Can I apply for B.Tech if I appeared in JEE Main but did not qualify?
A: Yes, you can appear in the GITM-ET (institution's own entrance test).
   There is no JEE Main qualifying score requirement — JEE score is used
   for merit ranking if available.

Q: What is the difference between B.Tech CSE and B.Tech AI & Data Science?
A: B.Tech CSE is a broader programme covering all areas of computer science
   with an optional AI/ML specialization in the final year.
   B.Tech AI & Data Science (AIDS) is a specialized programme focused exclusively
   on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Data Science from the first year.

Q: Can I join M.Tech without a GATE score?
A: Yes, GATE score is preferred but not mandatory for M.Tech admission.
   However, GATE score holders receive scholarship preference.

FEES AND SCHOLARSHIPS QUESTIONS
=============================================================

Q: What is the total fee for B.Tech?
A: The approximate total fee for a 4-year B.Tech programme is INR 6,32,000
   (approximately INR 1,58,000 per year — sample amounts). This includes
   tuition, development, exam, library, and laboratory fees.

Q: Are scholarships available?
A: Yes. Several scholarships are available including merit scholarships
   (up to 100% tuition waiver for top rankers), need-based financial assistance,
   and facilitated government scholarship applications. See the Scholarships document.

Q: Is education loan assistance available?
A: Yes. The institution has tie-ups with SBI, Bank of Baroda, and Canara Bank
   for education loans. The Finance Office can assist with documentation.

Q: What is the last date to apply for scholarship?
A: Institution scholarships must be applied for within 30 days of joining.
   Government scholarships through NSP (National Scholarship Portal) must be
   applied by October 31 each year.

DOCUMENTS QUESTIONS
=============================================================

Q: What documents are required for admission?
A: Key documents include: Class 10 and 12 mark sheets and certificates,
   Transfer Certificate, Community Certificate (if applicable),
   Income Certificate (for fee concession), Aadhar Card, 6 passport photographs.
   See the Documents Required guide for the complete list.

Q: Can I submit photocopies during application?
A: Scanned copies are accepted during the online application.
   Original documents must be produced at the time of document verification.

Q: My Transfer Certificate hasn't arrived yet. Can I still apply?
A: Yes, you can apply without the Transfer Certificate.
   However, you must produce the TC at the time of final document verification.
   Without TC submission, admission cannot be confirmed.

HOSTEL AND CAMPUS QUESTIONS
=============================================================

Q: Is hostel accommodation available on campus?
A: Yes. On-campus hostel is available for both male and female students.
   Rooms are available in non-AC and AC variants (twin sharing and single).

Q: What is the hostel fee?
A: Non-AC twin sharing: INR 40,000/year; Non-AC single: INR 55,000/year;
   AC twin sharing: INR 65,000/year (sample amounts). Mess is approximately
   INR 3,500 per month.

Q: Is hostel accommodation guaranteed?
A: Hostel allotment is based on availability and is done on merit order.
   Not all applicants are guaranteed hostel accommodation.
