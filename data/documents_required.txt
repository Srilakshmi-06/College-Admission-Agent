DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR ADMISSION — SAMPLE GUIDE
Greenfield Institute of Technology and Management
Academic Year 2024-2025

NOTE: This is a SAMPLE/DEMO document. Replace with your institution's official document.

=============================================================
DOCUMENTS REQUIRED AT TIME OF APPLICATION
=============================================================

All applicants must submit scanned copies of the following documents
when applying online:

1. Class 10 (SSLC/Matriculation) Mark Sheet
   - Clear scan of original mark sheet
   - All subjects and final grade clearly visible

2. Class 12 (HSC/Intermediate) Mark Sheet
   - All year mark sheets if available
   - Final board examination mark sheet mandatory

3. Class 10 Passing Certificate
4. Class 12 Passing Certificate (or Provisional Certificate)

5. Transfer Certificate (TC)
   - From the last institution attended
   - Must be in original at the time of document verification

6. Migration Certificate (if applicable)
   - Required if the student is from a different state board

7. Community Certificate (for SC/ST/OBC/EWS students)
   - Issued by the competent authority (Tahsildar / District Collector)
   - Must be in the name of the student

8. Income Certificate (for scholarship / fee concession applicants)
   - Annual family income certificate from competent authority

9. Aadhar Card / National ID
   - Self-attested copy

10. Passport-size Photographs
    - 6 recent passport-size colour photographs (white background)

11. JEE Main Scorecard (if applicable)
    - Required for JEE-based merit admission

12. GATE Scorecard (for M.Tech applicants, if applicable)

=============================================================
DOCUMENTS REQUIRED AT TIME OF ORIGINAL VERIFICATION
=============================================================

On the day of document verification and admission confirmation,
students must bring ORIGINALS along with TWO sets of self-attested photocopies:

1. All mark sheets (Class 10 and 12 originals)
2. Passing / Completion Certificates (originals)
3. Transfer Certificate (original — to be surrendered)
4. Migration Certificate (original — to be surrendered)
5. Community Certificate (original)
6. Income Certificate (original, if applicable)
7. Conduct / Character Certificate from previous institution
8. Aadhar Card (original + photocopy)
9. Passport-size photographs (6 copies)
10. Bank Account Passbook / Cancelled Cheque (for scholarship disbursement)
11. Medical Fitness Certificate from a registered medical practitioner
12. Anti-Ragging Undertaking Form (provided at the time of admission)
    - Signed by student AND parent/guardian

=============================================================
ADDITIONAL DOCUMENTS FOR SPECIFIC CATEGORIES
=============================================================

NRI Students:
- Valid Passport copy
- Visa / Residency proof
- Equivalency certificate from AIU (Association of Indian Universities)

Physically Challenged Students (PwD):
- Disability Certificate from civil surgeon or medical board
- Minimum 40% disability for reservation benefit

Sports Quota Applicants:
- Sports achievement certificates from recognised federation
- Graded sportsperson certificate (if applicable)

=============================================================
POSTGRADUATE SPECIFIC DOCUMENTS
=============================================================

M.Tech Applicants:
- B.Tech / B.E. Degree Certificate or Final Year Provisional Certificate
- All semester mark sheets from undergraduate programme
- GATE Scorecard (if claiming GATE scholarship)

MBA Applicants:
- Undergraduate Degree Certificate
- All mark sheets from undergraduate programme
- CAT/MAT/XAT Scorecard (if claiming merit)

=============================================================
IMPORTANT NOTES ON DOCUMENTS
=============================================================

1. All documents must be genuine. Submission of forged documents will
   result in immediate cancellation of admission and legal action.

2. Original documents will be verified on admission day. Do not submit originals
   by post — bring them in person.

3. Incomplete documentation at the time of verification may result in
   deferral or cancellation of the admission offer.

4. This is a SAMPLE document. Always refer to the official admission notification
   for the current year's document requirements.
